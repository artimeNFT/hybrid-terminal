## Semantics and implementation

- **TP2 is the vector-zone midpoint.** TP1 is near-edge touch only, without partial exit. E is the confirmed decision close, never the latest developing price or a selected journal entry.
- Main after-assumption payoff is `(d × (TP2−E)−F)/D`, **original risk** `D=abs(E−S)`. It is not a new net reward/risk ratio using an expanded stop loss, expected value or probability. Literal E100, LONG S98/T106 or SHORT S102/T94 gives gross3, fee10bps payoff2.95, ordinary stop−1.05. Gap100bps gives−1.54 LONG/−1.56 SHORT. Fixed baseline costR stays0.05; it is neither overwritten nor deducted again. E100/S99/T101, fee100bps gives0; adding100bps slippage gives−1, while gross stays1 and eligibility is unchanged.
- The main summary names the actual fee/slippage assumptions; gap assumption affects only stressed stop. Invalid/empty inputs immediately clear scenario summary and stop output, while valid gross geometry remains. Per-metric ATR/baseline guards stay separate. WAIT/loading/invalid confirmed geometry clears old prices, path, scenario and nominal output; original settings remain accessible. No alternative-side/developing plan is invented.
- Original calculation, selection/eligibility, targets/stops, `plannedRiskDisplay`, `planningCostScenario`, nominal sizing, target-path geometry/distances, feed, journal/export math and rule/execution/RSI versions remain exact. Only two preexisting UI composers change: `panel` and `updatePlanningCosts`. New read-only helpers update fragments and preserve expanded/focused target-path/sizing details. No framework or production engine refactor.
- Workspace, cost/sizing inputs and their listeners mount once. Only read-only fragments change; static expanders, focused number inputs and browser-native partial typing such as `1e` survive provisional/confirmed updates, forced normal renders and source transitions. Cost edits/reset update main and detail together through the original handler, without recomputing strategy. Original storage keys/defaults/guards remain; no additional preference, network call, timer, dependency or journal write.
- Nominal budget excludes fees/slippage/gaps, is not maximum loss or executable quantity, and preserves original Spot cash versus unconfigured perpetual margin treatment. Planning is decision-close; historical simulation enters next open. Funding/spread/latency/account effects remain unmodeled/unverified, never a fully adjusted-return claim. SHORT remains research/simulation, not approved Spot short execution.
